Dead Air Privacy Policy

Applies to the Dead Air and Dead Air Beta apps for Atlassian Cloud. Effective November 11, 2026.

Dead Air watches Jira Service Management desks for work that has quietly stopped, such as a request type that went silent or a recurring task that was not done. This page explains what the app stores, where it stores it, and how to have it removed.

Where your data lives

Dead Air is built on Atlassian Forge and runs entirely on Atlassian's platform. It makes no calls to any server outside Atlassian, and the app's developer does not receive copies of your Jira or Confluence content. Everything the app stores is kept in Atlassian's app storage for your own site.

What the app reads

To do its job, Dead Air reads, through Atlassian's APIs and only on sites where it is installed:

  • issues and service desk requests on desks you choose to watch, including their dates, request types and status;
  • the last-edited date of Confluence pages linked from incidents, if the Confluence part is installed;
  • whether the person watching a desk still has access to it.

Dead Air stores no names, email addresses, passwords or ticket text. When it opens an alert issue on your desk, that issue lives in your Jira like any other ticket and follows your site's own retention.

Personal data

The only personal data Dead Air stores is the Atlassian account ID described above. Atlassian requires apps that store account IDs to report them to its personal data reporting service and to delete or update them when an account is closed or changed. Dead Air is adding this reporting during the beta. Until it is live, email the address below and any stored account ID will be removed.

Removing your data

Stopping a watch from the Dead Air board deletes that desk's watch record and schedules its scan history for deletion. Uninstalling the app removes it from your site. To ask for deletion of anything the app stored, or with any question about this policy, email 7kevinmasterson@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
